New in my kit: the BEYOND Performer Console

My laser setup just got a proper hands-on controller: the BEYOND Performer Console from Pangolin Laser Systems. From now on it runs the lasers in my live shows.

16 October 2026 · Peter Van Engelandt

What it is

The Performer Console is a MIDI controller made for Pangolin's BEYOND laser software. It is laid out the way BEYOND already works, so the important functions sit under my fingers instead of behind a mouse.

  • Four control layers, so one surface covers far more than its size suggests.
  • Direct access to BEYOND plugins: colour picker, custom workspace, perfect delays, and zone flips and selections.
  • Ready to go: Pangolin supplies a setup file, a zone file and a MIDI map, and a free MIDI Mapping course to customise the layout with scripting.
  • Built for the road: it comes with a carrying case and a USB-C cable.

Why I chose it

Lasers are at their best when they are played, not just triggered. A mouse is fine for building content, but on show night I want to react to the music and the room: bring a beam in slowly, hold a symmetrical look, then hit hard on the drop.

With a dedicated surface I can:

  • Busk live with the same feel as my lighting desk.
  • Control zones and colours instantly, without hunting through menus.
  • Keep the look tight and deliberate: slow, controlled movement, strong hits, clean symmetrical shapes in a dark room.

In my future shows

The BEYOND Performer Console is now part of my standard kit for laser design and operation at events. If you are planning a show with lasers, lighting and video from one designer, get in touch.
