About

Peter Van Engelandt

I am an independent lighting, laser and video specialist based in Wevelgem, Belgium, working with event agencies, venues, production companies and organisations throughout Belgium and beyond.

My workflow is built on industry-standard tools: grandMA3 and ChamSys MQ500M+ consoles for control, Pangolin Beyond Ultimate for laser, Resolume Arena for video, and Vectorworks, Capture and Unreal Engine 5 with Yunsio SuperStage for design and previsualisation.

Every project starts with listening. By understanding the purpose of your event, I create visual experiences that elevate the atmosphere, put the spotlight where it belongs and run with complete technical reliability.
